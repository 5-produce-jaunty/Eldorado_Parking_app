# Eldorado Community App — Board Test Guide

Thanks for helping test our new community app! It's an early **prototype** that
replaces the download-print-email forms with simple in-app requests that the
board approves online.

**Open it on your phone or computer:**
**https://5-produce-jaunty.github.io/Eldorado_Parking_app/**

### Please read first
- This is a **demo with made-up sample data** — no real resident info.
- It **resets every time you refresh** the page, and **no real emails are
  sent**. That's expected; just try the flows and tell us what you think.
- There's **no login yet**. To play different people, use the **role dropdown
  in the top-right corner** (e.g., switch between a resident and a board member).

---

## What to try

### 1. As a resident (pick e.g. "Maria Alvarez" in the top-right dropdown)
Use the tabs across the top:
- **Request permit** — request an overnight **guest parking** pass (it's only for
  visitors; enter the guest's plate and dates).
- **Reservations** — reserve the **pool gazebo** for a party. Notice it enforces
  our rules automatically (max 28 people, 3 hours, ends by 8 PM, 2 weeks ahead,
  $150 deposit acknowledgment).
- **ARC** — submit an **architectural / landscape review** request (fence, paint,
  landscaping, etc.). Paint colors are limited to our approved palette. You can
  attach photos/plans.

### 2. As a board member (switch the dropdown to "Dolores Reyes", "Tom Becker", or "Frank Lai")
- Go to the **Admin** tab at the bottom, then **Approvals**.
- You'll see pending **guest permits**, **pool parties**, and **architectural**
  requests in one place.

**Architectural requests now need a board majority (2 of 3).**
To test it:
1. As **Dolores**, open Approvals → an ARC request → tap **Approve** (add a note).
2. Switch the top-right dropdown to **Tom** → Approvals → same request → tap
   **Approve**.
3. Once two of the three board members approve, the request is **automatically
   approved** and the resident is notified. (Two denials would deny it; you can
   also "Approve with conditions" or "Request more info," which pauses the clock.)

Parking and pool requests are approved by a single board action (tap Approve/Deny).

### 3. As patrol (the "Patrol" tab — available to Dolores)
- Look up a license plate to see permit status and any violations, and **log a
  violation** (you can attach a photo).
